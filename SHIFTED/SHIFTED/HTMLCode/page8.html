    Before You Sign

    Buying Tips

    Always test drive before committing. Check comfort, visibility and how the car handles at both low and higher speeds.

    Check what is covered, for how long, and whether it transfers if you buy the car used.

    For used cars, ask for service records to understand how consistently the vehicle has been maintained.

    Have an independent workshop inspect any used car before purchase to catch issues early.

    Compare quotes from multiple insurers — premiums can vary significantly for the same vehicle.
