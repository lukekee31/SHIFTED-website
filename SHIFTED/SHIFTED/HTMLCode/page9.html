Car Maintenance Guide

Looking after your car doesn't have to be complicated. Learn the essential maintenance tasks that improve safety, reliability and long-term ownership.

Why It Matters

Why Maintenance Matters

Routine maintenance prevents breakdowns, improves safety, saves money over time, maintains vehicle performance and protects resale value. A few small habits go a long way toward reliable ownership.

Know the Signs

Warning Signs

Engine Warning Light

Can indicate anything from a loose fuel cap to a serious engine fault — get it scanned promptly.

Smoke from Exhaust

Blue, white or black smoke can point to oil burning, coolant leaks or fuel issues respectively.

Strange Noises

Squealing, grinding or knocking sounds often signal worn brakes, belts or engine components.

Vibrations While Driving

May indicate unbalanced tyres, worn suspension components or brake issues.

By Powertrain

Petrol vs Hybrid vs Electric Maintenance

Petrol
  • More frequent oil changes
  • More moving parts
  • Regular engine servicing
Hybrid
  • Petrol engine plus battery
  • Regenerative braking reduces brake wear
  • Slightly more complex servicing
Electric
  • No engine oil
  • Fewer moving parts
  • Battery and software inspections
Rules of Thumb

Quick Tips

Check tyre pressure once a month.
Don't ignore dashboard warning lights.
Follow your manufacturer's service schedule.
Keep your battery terminals clean.
