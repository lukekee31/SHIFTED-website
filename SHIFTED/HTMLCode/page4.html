Hybrid vs Electric

Both hybrid and electric vehicles are shaping the future of transportation. Learn how they work, where they differ, and which may be the better choice depending on your driving needs.

Two Paths to Electrification

Why It Matters

Electrification is becoming more common as manufacturers respond to environmental considerations and evolving driver expectations. There isn't a single "best" solution — different technologies suit different drivers.

Hybrid Vehicle

Petrol Engine → Battery → Electric Motor → Wheels

  • A petrol engine provides the primary power source.
  • A small battery stores energy for electric-only driving at low speeds.
  • An electric motor assists the engine to improve efficiency.
  • Regenerative braking recovers energy that would otherwise be lost.

Electric Vehicle

Battery Pack → Inverter → Electric Motor → Wheels

  • A large battery pack stores all of the energy needed to drive.
  • An inverter converts stored energy for the electric motor.
  • The electric motor delivers power directly to the wheels.
  • No petrol engine or exhaust system is required.
Weighing It Up

Advantages & Disadvantages

Hybrid

Advantages

  • Better fuel economy
  • No charging required
  • Lower emissions

Disadvantages

  • More complex mechanically
  • Still uses petrol
  • Smaller battery capacity

Electric

Advantages

  • Zero tailpipe emissions
  • Instant torque
  • Quiet operation

Disadvantages

  • Charging required
  • Longer refuelling time
  • Battery degradation over time
