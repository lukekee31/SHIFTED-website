Hybrid vs Electric

Both hybrid and electric vehicles are shaping the future of transportation. Learn how they work, where they differ, and which may be the better choice depending on your driving needs.

A Balanced View

Two paths toward cleaner driving

Electrification is becoming more common as manufacturers respond to environmental concerns and improving battery technology. Neither hybrid nor electric vehicles are universally "better" — each solves different problems for different drivers.

Hybrid Vehicle

Petrol Engine Battery Electric Motor Wheels

  • A petrol engine works alongside an electric motor.
  • A small battery stores energy for electric-only driving.
  • Regenerative braking recovers energy when slowing down.
  • No charging cable is required.

Electric Vehicle

Battery Inverter Motor Wheels

  • A large battery pack stores all the vehicle's energy.
  • An inverter converts stored energy for the electric motor.
  • The motor delivers instant, smooth torque to the wheels.
  • Charging is required to replenish the battery.
Weigh It Up

Advantages & Disadvantages

Hybrid

Advantages

  • Better fuel economy
  • No charging required
  • Lower emissions than petrol

Disadvantages

  • More mechanically complex
  • Still uses petrol
  • Smaller battery capacity

Electric

Advantages

  • Zero tailpipe emissions
  • Instant torque
  • Quiet operation

Disadvantages

  • Charging required
  • Longer refuelling time
  • Battery degrades over time
