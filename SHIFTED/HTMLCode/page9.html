Ownership Guide

Choosing the right car is about more than performance. Understanding your budget, lifestyle and driving habits will help you find the vehicle that best suits your needs.

Buying With Confidence

Buying a car is one of the largest financial decisions many people make. Beyond the purchase price, owners should consider fuel costs, maintenance, insurance, servicing and how the vehicle fits their daily driving needs.

Whether you choose a petrol, hybrid or electric vehicle depends on your priorities rather than a single "best" option.

Buying Checklist

Budget

Consider the purchase price, financing and insurance.

Daily Driving Distance

Short city trips may favour hybrids or EVs. Long highway journeys may favour petrol or hybrid vehicles.

Charging Availability

Do you have convenient access to home or public charging?

Passenger Capacity

Choose a vehicle appropriate for your family's needs.

Storage Space

Consider luggage and cargo requirements.

Maintenance Costs

Research servicing schedules and long-term ownership costs.

Running Costs

Owning a car involves more than the initial purchase. Everyday running costs typically include:

  • Fuel or Electricity — petrol at the pump, or electricity at home or public chargers.
  • Insurance — required coverage that varies with vehicle type and driver profile.
  • Servicing — routine maintenance to keep the vehicle safe and reliable.
  • Road Tax — an annual cost that can vary by engine type and emissions.
  • Tyres — replaced periodically as tread wears down.

Maintenance Tips

Engine Oil

Change according to manufacturer recommendations.

Tyres

Maintain correct tyre pressure and inspect tread regularly.

Brakes

Inspect brake pads and discs for wear.

Battery

For hybrids and EVs, monitor battery health and follow servicing guidelines.

Fluids

Check coolant, brake fluid and washer fluid regularly.
