Car Maintenance Guide

Looking after your car doesn't have to be complicated. Learn the essential maintenance tasks that improve safety, reliability and long-term ownership.

Why It Matters

Small habits, big benefits

Routine maintenance prevents breakdowns, improves safety, and saves money over the life of the vehicle. It also helps maintain performance and protects resale value — all for a fraction of the cost of major repairs.

Pay Attention

Warning Signs

Engine Warning Light

Often signals a sensor fault or emissions issue — have it checked promptly.

Smoke from Exhaust

Blue, white or black smoke can indicate burning oil, coolant leaks or fuel issues.

Strange Noises

Squealing, grinding or knocking sounds often signal wear that needs attention.

Vibrations While Driving

Can point to unbalanced wheels, worn suspension or brake issues.

Powertrain Differences

Petrol vs Hybrid vs Electric Maintenance

Petrol

  • More frequent oil changes
  • More moving parts
  • Regular engine servicing

Hybrid

  • Petrol engine plus battery
  • Regenerative braking reduces brake wear
  • Slightly more complex servicing

Electric

  • No engine oil
  • Fewer moving parts
  • Battery and software inspections
Remember

Quick Tips

Check tyre pressure once a month.
Don't ignore dashboard warning lights.
Follow your manufacturer's service schedule.
Keep your battery terminals clean.
