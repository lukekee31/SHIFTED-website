Singapore's Motorsport Scene

Singapore may be a small country, but it hosts one of the world's most iconic street races while continuing to support regional motorsport through international championships and grassroots racing.

A Racing Nation

Passion beyond the podium

Since 2008, the Singapore Grand Prix has placed the country firmly on the global motorsport map. Beyond Formula One, Singapore continues to support regional championships that attract engineers, teams and enthusiasts from across Asia.

Championships

Featured Motorsport Series

Formula One

The pinnacle of open-wheel racing, hosted in Singapore since 2008.

FIA World Endurance Championship

Gruelling endurance racing including the legendary Le Mans 24 Hours.

IMSA SportsCar Championship

North America's premier sports car racing series.

GT World Challenge Asia

A regional GT series featuring manufacturers from across the world.

Porsche Carrera Cup Asia

A one-make series showcasing the Porsche 911 GT3 Cup car.

Ferrari Challenge Asia Pacific

A one-make Ferrari series popular with regional collectors and drivers.

Formula One

Race duration: Approx. 1.5–2 hours

Famous manufacturers: Ferrari, Mercedes, McLaren

Formula One is the world's premier single-seater racing series, and the Singapore Grand Prix is one of its most iconic events.

FIA World Endurance Championship

Race duration: Up to 24 hours

Famous manufacturers: Porsche, Ferrari, Toyota, Peugeot

The WEC tests both car and driver over extreme distances, including the legendary 24 Hours of Le Mans.

IMSA SportsCar Championship

Race duration: Ranges from 1 hour to 24 hours (Daytona)

Famous manufacturers: Cadillac, Porsche, Acura, BMW

North America's leading sports car series, famous for the Rolex 24 at Daytona.

GT World Challenge Asia

Race duration: Approx. 1 hour per race

Famous manufacturers: Mercedes-AMG, BMW, Porsche, Audi

A leading regional GT3 series that regularly features on the Asian racing calendar.

Porsche Carrera Cup Asia

Race duration: Approx. 30–40 minutes

Famous cars: Porsche 911 GT3 Cup

A one-make series that gives drivers identical machinery, putting the focus purely on skill.

Ferrari Challenge Asia Pacific

Race duration: Approx. 30 minutes

Famous cars: Ferrari 296 Challenge

A one-make Ferrari series popular with regional collectors, gentleman drivers and rising talent.

Milestones

Singapore Motorsport Timeline

2008

First Singapore Formula One Grand Prix

Singapore hosts F1's first-ever night race, transforming Marina Bay into a global racing icon.

2012

Growth of GT Racing

Regional GT championships expand, drawing manufacturers and teams from across Asia.

2020+

Electric Racing Expansion

Interest grows in electric and hybrid racing series as manufacturers shift focus.

Future

Sustainable Motorsport

Racing continues to evolve toward more sustainable fuels and powertrains.

Summary

More than entertainment

Motorsport is more than a spectacle — it develops the technology that eventually reaches everyday road cars. Singapore continues to play an important role in international motorsport, hosting world-class events while supporting the growth of regional racing series.
