From practical family sedans to modern electric vehicles, discover the cars that have become favourites among Singaporean drivers.

The Singapore Context

A unique car ownership environment

Owning a car in Singapore is different from most countries. The Certificate of Entitlement (COE) system, combined with high overall vehicle costs, makes reliability, efficiency and resale value especially important considerations for local buyers.

Spotlight

Manufacturers Behind the Wheel

Toyota

Founded in 1937, Toyota is renowned worldwide for reliability and pioneering hybrid technology. Its models consistently rank among Singapore's best-selling cars thanks to strong resale value and low running costs.

Honda

Honda has built a reputation for engaging, well-engineered cars since 1948. Its Civic and HR-V models are common sights on Singapore roads, prized for their driving feel and build quality.

BYD

BYD has rapidly become one of the world's largest EV makers, and its growing lineup in Singapore offers strong value alongside competitive range and technology.
