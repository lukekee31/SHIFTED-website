Owning a Car Here

Singapore's Unique Car Market

Car ownership in Singapore is shaped by the Certificate of Entitlement (COE) system, which limits the number of vehicles on the road and adds significantly to purchase cost. As a result, reliability, fuel efficiency and long-term running costs matter more to Singaporean buyers than almost anywhere else.

Brand Focus

Manufacturer Spotlight

Toyota

Founded in Japan in 1937, Toyota has built a global reputation for reliability and innovation, including pioneering hybrid technology with the Prius.

Why it's popular in Singapore: Strong resale value, low maintenance costs, and a wide dealer network.

Honda

Honda has long been known for engineering excellence, particularly in engine technology, producing some of the most beloved sedans and hatchbacks in the world.

Why it's popular in Singapore: Sporty driving character combined with everyday reliability.

BYD

A fast-growing Chinese manufacturer specialising in electric vehicles and battery technology, rapidly expanding its presence across Southeast Asia.

Why it's popular in Singapore: Competitive pricing for electric vehicles relative to rivals.
