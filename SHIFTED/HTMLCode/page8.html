Electric Power

Driving without combustion. Electric vehicles replace the traditional engine with powerful electric motors and high-capacity batteries, delivering smooth, quiet and highly efficient transportation.

Power Without a Petrol Tank

Electric vehicles use electricity stored inside a battery pack instead of burning petrol or diesel. The stored electrical energy powers one or more electric motors that drive the wheels.

Because there is no combustion process, electric vehicles produce zero tailpipe emissions while delivering instant torque and quiet operation.

What is an Electric Vehicle?

Electric vehicles store energy inside a rechargeable battery pack. When the accelerator is pressed, electricity flows through an inverter into the electric motor, producing instant torque that turns the wheels.

Main Components

Battery Pack

Stores electrical energy using lithium-ion cells at high voltage. It is the largest and heaviest component in an EV.

Electric Motor

Converts electricity into mechanical motion, delivering instant torque with very few moving parts and high efficiency.

Inverter

Converts DC electricity from the battery into AC electricity for the motor, while also controlling motor speed.

Charging Port

Allows the vehicle to recharge using external power, supporting both AC and DC charging depending on the vehicle.

Charging Methods

Home Charging (AC)

Typical Power: 7–11 kW

Charging Time: 6–10 hours. Best for overnight charging.

Public AC Charging

Typical Power: 11–22 kW

Common at shopping centres and workplaces.

DC Fast Charging

Typical Power: 50–350 kW

Recharges many EVs from around 10% to 80% in roughly 20–40 minutes.

Charging Speed Comparison

Charging times vary depending on battery size, charger power and environmental conditions.

Advantages

Zero tailpipe emissions

Instant torque

Quiet driving

Lower maintenance

High energy efficiency

Disadvantages

Charging takes longer than refuelling

Limited charging infrastructure in some areas

Higher purchase price

Battery degradation over time

Longer-distance travel requires planning

Summary

Electric vehicles represent a major shift in automotive technology. By replacing combustion engines with electric motors and battery packs, they offer quieter operation, instant acceleration and zero tailpipe emissions. As charging infrastructure continues to improve, EVs are becoming an increasingly practical choice for many drivers.
