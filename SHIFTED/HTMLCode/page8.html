Car Buying Guide

Buying a car is one of the biggest financial decisions many people make. Learn what to consider before choosing the vehicle that's right for you.

    Good Practice

    Buying Tips

    Always test drive on varied roads, including highways and rougher surfaces, to properly judge comfort and noise levels.

    Check what's covered, for how long, and whether the warranty transfers if you sell the car before it expires.

    For used cars, request full service records to understand how well the vehicle has been looked after.

    Have an independent inspection done before buying a used car to catch issues that aren't visible at first glance.

    Compare quotes from multiple insurers, as premiums can vary significantly based on the vehicle and your driving profile.
