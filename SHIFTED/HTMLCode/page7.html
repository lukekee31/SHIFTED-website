Ferrari F40

Manufacturer: Ferrari  |  Country of Origin: Italy

Engine/Motor: 2.9L Twin-Turbo V8  |  Power: 471 hp

The F40 was the last car personally approved by Enzo Ferrari.

McLaren P1

Manufacturer: McLaren  |  Country of Origin: United Kingdom

Engine/Motor: 3.8L Twin-Turbo V8 Hybrid  |  Power: 903 hp

The P1 combines a petrol engine with an electric motor for extra performance.

Lamborghini Aventador

Manufacturer: Lamborghini  |  Country of Origin: Italy

Engine/Motor: 6.5L Naturally Aspirated V12  |  Power: 700 hp

The Aventador's V12 traces its design lineage back to Lamborghini's very first V12 engine.

Porsche 911

Manufacturer: Porsche  |  Country of Origin: Germany

Engine/Motor: 3.0L Twin-Turbo Flat-Six  |  Power: 379 hp

The 911 has kept its distinctive silhouette for over 60 years.

Toyota GR Supra

Manufacturer: Toyota  |  Country of Origin: Japan

Engine/Motor: 3.0L Turbo Inline-Six  |  Power: 382 hp

The GR Supra was co-developed with BMW, sharing its platform with the Z4.

Nissan Z

Manufacturer: Nissan  |  Country of Origin: Japan

Engine/Motor: 3.0L Twin-Turbo V6  |  Power: 400 hp

The Nissan Z pays visual tribute to the original 240Z from 1969.

Nissan GT-R R35

Manufacturer: Nissan  |  Country of Origin: Japan

Engine/Motor: 3.8L Twin-Turbo V6  |  Power: 565 hp

Nicknamed 'Godzilla' for its dominance on track.

Toyota AE86

Manufacturer: Toyota  |  Country of Origin: Japan

Engine/Motor: 1.6L Naturally Aspirated Inline-Four  |  Power: 128 hp

The AE86 became a motorsport icon thanks to its lightweight, balanced chassis.

Honda NSX

Manufacturer: Honda  |  Country of Origin: Japan

Engine/Motor: 3.5L Twin-Turbo V6 Hybrid  |  Power: 573 hp

The original NSX was developed with input from F1 driver Ayrton Senna.

Porsche Taycan

Manufacturer: Porsche  |  Country of Origin: Germany

The Taycan was Porsche's first mass-production all-electric car.

Tesla Model S Plaid

Manufacturer: Tesla  |  Country of Origin: United States

Engine/Motor: Tri-Motor Electric  |  Power: 1,020 hp

The Model S Plaid is among the quickest-accelerating production cars ever built.

Hyundai IONIQ 5 N

Manufacturer: Hyundai  |  Country of Origin: South Korea

Engine/Motor: Dual Electric Motors  |  Power: 641 hp

Featured Car of the Week

Porsche 911 GT3 RS

Built as a track-focused evolution of the standard 911, the GT3 RS pairs a high-revving naturally aspirated flat-six with extensive aerodynamic upgrades borrowed directly from motorsport.

Why it's iconic: It represents the purest expression of the 911 bloodline — uncompromising, track-honed, and still road legal.

Signature features: Naturally aspirated flat-six engine, adjustable rear wing, and motorsport-derived suspension geometry.
