Hybrid Power

The best of both worlds. Hybrid vehicles combine a petrol engine with an electric motor to improve fuel efficiency while maintaining the convenience of traditional refuelling.

Two Power Sources, One Vehicle

Hybrid vehicles combine two different power sources: a petrol engine and an electric motor. Depending on driving conditions, either system can power the vehicle individually or both systems can work together to maximise efficiency and performance.

Unlike fully electric vehicles, hybrids never rely entirely on external charging because the battery can recharge itself while driving.

What is a Hybrid?

A hybrid vehicle intelligently switches between petrol power, electric power or both depending on speed, battery charge and driving conditions. This improves fuel economy while reducing emissions.

Hybrid System Diagram

Energy flows between the petrol engine, electric motor and battery depending on driving conditions, with the power control unit deciding which source to use at any given moment.

Main Components

Petrol Engine

Provides power during higher-speed driving and charges the battery when required.

Electric Motor

Provides instant torque and assists the petrol engine during acceleration.

Hybrid Battery

Stores electrical energy for the motor. Hybrid batteries are much smaller than those used in fully electric vehicles.

Power Control Unit

Acts as the "brain" of the hybrid system by deciding when to use petrol power, electric power or both.

Regenerative Braking

When the driver slows down, the electric motor acts as a generator instead of consuming electricity. The vehicle's kinetic energy is converted into electrical energy and stored in the battery rather than being wasted as heat through the brakes.

  • Improves efficiency
  • Reduces brake wear
  • Recharges the battery

Summary

Hybrid vehicles bridge the gap between traditional petrol cars and fully electric vehicles. By combining a petrol engine with an electric motor, hybrids improve fuel efficiency, reduce emissions and deliver smoother driving without requiring drivers to depend entirely on charging infrastructure.
